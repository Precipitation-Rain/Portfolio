Adverse Event Ranking System (AERS)

Drug safety signal scoring with analyst review workflow

Processes 4M+ FDA adverse event reports and scores 388K+ drug-reaction pairs to find potential safety issues. Each pair is scored on whether the reaction is already known (checked live via the FDA API), how severe it is, and how often it's reported. Analysts can then confirm, dismiss, or escalate each signal through a Streamlit dashboard.

US Road Accidents — 7.7M EDA Study

Finding patterns in 7.7 million US traffic accident records

Analyzed 7.7 million accident records across 49 states to find when, where, and why accidents happen. Key findings: California alone accounts for 33% of all US accidents, most accidents happen in clear weather (because there are more cars on the road), and nighttime accidents are less frequent but more severe.
